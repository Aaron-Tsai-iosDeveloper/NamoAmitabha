Hello,
I am a Buddhist disciple from Taiwan. In 2025, I developed this Buddha-recitation app with the help of AI.

My intention in creating it was to use **speech-recognition technology so that you need not be distracted by counting, and can gather body and mind into each utterance of the Buddha's name.**

The speech-recognition model currently supports 7 pronunciations for recitation:
**Chinese, Taiwanese, Japanese, Korean, Vietnamese, English, and Sanskrit**

**The model is not perfectly accurate.** Especially on first use, in noisy environments, or when speech is fast and unclear, counting errors may occur. I hope you can understand the app's shortcomings.

If you find this app helpful, **I would be grateful if you could leave a recommendation on the Play Store, App Store, or Meta Store,** so that more people have the chance to discover **Amitabha Buddha**.
Thank you!
